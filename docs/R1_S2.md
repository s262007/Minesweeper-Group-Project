UART v2 interface in `docs/CONTRACT.md`. It does not record team approval or a
board selection. Work proceeds one step at a time; wait for the user's go-ahead
before starting each next step. Do not modify R2 or R3 implementations, and do
not add entries to `docs/AI_USAGE.md`.
